FAQ

Good questions

Something else on your mind? Ask in the form below and I'll reply within a day.

How far in advance should I book? +

For weddings, 9–12 months is ideal, especially for summer Saturdays. Family and portrait sessions usually book 2–4 weeks ahead.

What if it rains on the day? +

For outdoor sessions we can move to the studio or pick a new date for free. For weddings I always plan a cosy indoor backup with you.

Do you edit every photo? +

Yes. Every photo in your gallery is hand-picked and colour-edited. Detailed retouching (like removing a passing stranger) is available on request.

How do we get our photos? +

In a private online gallery where you can download full-resolution files, share with family and order prints.

Do you travel? +

Travel within 30 miles of Lakeport is included. Destination weddings are very welcome; travel costs are added to your quote.
